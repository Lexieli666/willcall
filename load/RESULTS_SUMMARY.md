## What is not measured

Listed so its absence is a decision rather than an oversight.

- **Nothing on AWS.** No credentials on the build host. The Terraform is written and validated;
  `terraform plan` has never run.
- **No production propagation measurement.** The 5,000-connection figure comes from the load
  scenario. The server publishes its own flush duration; the client half is not instrumented.
- **No Interaction to Next Paint.** It is a field metric needing real sessions. Total Blocking
  Time, its lab proxy, is reported instead and is 0 ms.
- **No screen-reader verification.** Ten manual checks are listed as not done in
  `docs/accessibility.md`.
- **No real users.** The public demo has not happened, so [ADR 0012](../docs/adr/0012-hold-ttl.md)
  stays `proposed` and the hold TTL remains an unvalidated default.
